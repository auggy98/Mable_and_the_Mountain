🍎 Mable and the Mountain

📖 The Story & Rules (Click to hide/show)

Mable stole a basket of enchanted golden apples to help her escape the mountain guards. On her way down:

  • 1st guard got half her apples, plus 1 for good luck.
  • 2nd guard got half her remaining apples, plus 2 for good luck.
  • Repeats for the nth guard, who gets half her remaining apples, plus n.

At the bottom of the mountain, she had exactly 1 golden apple left.

👉 Start at the END (Right/Bottom) and work backwards to the START! 👈
